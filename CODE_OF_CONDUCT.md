# Community conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Critique ideas and code constructively. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the repository's private reporting channel. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Appeals may be sent privately to another maintainer. Do not publish identifying details of a report.
